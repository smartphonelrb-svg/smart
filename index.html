LI-ION • 1 CELL • 4.2V CC/CV

Yadda za ka caji batirin 3.7V da wutar 5V

Kar a haɗa batirin lithium kai tsaye zuwa 5V. Yi amfani da TP4056—circuit na caji mai sarrafa halin yanzu da ƙarfin lantarki—domin ya caje cell ɗaya lafiya zuwa 4.2V.

Duba circuit ɗin Zaɓi caji mai kyau
⚠️

Muhimmin gargadi

Wannan circuit na cell lithium-ion 3.7V guda ɗaya ne kawai (4.2V lokacin da ya cika). Kada a yi amfani da shi ga batirin 7.4V/2S, batirin da ya kumbura, ko cell mara kariya ba tare da kariyar BMS ba.

Tsarin haɗawa

Circuit mai sauƙi da TP4056

Ana iya amfani da module TP4056 da ke da tashoshin B+/B− da OUT+/OUT−. Wannan shi ne mafi sauƙin hanyar ginawa.

1. Shigarwa

Haɗa 5V USB zuwa IN+, sannan ground zuwa IN−.

2. Batiri

Haɗa plus na batiri zuwa B+, minus kuma zuwa B−.

3. Na'ura

Idan module ɗin na da protection, haɗa na'urarka zuwa OUT+ da OUT−.

Jerin kaya

Abin da za ka tanada

  • 01TP4056 module mai protection (DW01A + 8205A idan akwai).
  • 02Batirin Li-ion 3.7V guda ɗaya, misali 18650 mai inganci.
  • 035V USB adapter mai iya bayar da isasshen current.
  • 04Waya, switch, da fuse (na zaɓi amma yana da kyau).
